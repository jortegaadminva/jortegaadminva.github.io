Marketplace & catalog operations for a US automotive parts manufacturer

California-based ECM and TIPM rebuilder, supported via Maks DataTech · Jan 2021 – Mar 2025

  • RoleE-Commerce Operations
  • Tools / PlatformsWooCommerce · eBay
  • FocusCatalog accuracy & vehicle fitment

The situation

An automotive parts manufacturer and rebuilder selling electronic control modules and replacement parts across WooCommerce and eBay, where listing accuracy and vehicle fitment data directly affect whether a buyer orders the right part.

What I handled

  • Created, optimized and maintained listings across WooCommerce (WordPress) and eBay — titles, descriptions, item specifics, SEO keywords, specifications, images, pricing and inventory
  • Maintained catalogs by updating SKU records, product specifications and vehicle compatibility (fitment) information across sales channels
  • Product research, competitor analysis and market analysis to support sourcing and pricing decisions
  • Edited, resized and optimized product images in Adobe Photoshop for consistent listing quality
  • Built and updated product pages using Elementor within WordPress
  • Monitored order workflows, shipping status and fulfillment records in ShipStation, and prepared sales and fulfillment reports

Tools & Platforms

  • WooCommerce
  • WordPress
  • Elementor
  • eBay Seller Hub
  • ShipStation
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Microsoft Excel

Why it mattered

Supported ongoing catalog, listing, fulfillment and reporting work across a large automotive product range, with attention to the listing and fitment accuracy that affects whether a buyer orders the right part.

  • Tools / PlatformsEtsy
  • FocusDaily storefront ops & fulfillment

The situation

An Etsy operation needed daily storefront management, dropshipping fulfillment coordinated with an overseas supplier, and stronger marketplace visibility through keyword-driven listing optimization.

What I handled

  • Daily storefront operations across multiple SKUs — product listings, catalog updates, inventory reconciliation, pricing records and order fulfillment
